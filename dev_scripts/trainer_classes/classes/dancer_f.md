# DANCER (dancer_f) — Bharatanatyam

- **Bases:** front head, face and torso from `psychic_f` (glow, ball and hair spike removed); both arms and hands blitted from `pokemon_breeder_f` (vanilla size, 3px narrower each side). The legs are a narrow aramandi built to vanilla limb widths. OW `lass` moved to **npc_2**.
- **Front palette:** warm brown skin 1-4, red silk 5-7, temple gold 8/9, black hair A/B/C, jasmine D/E.
- **Design:** natyarambhe arms (out at shoulder height, open hands). Elbow-length sleeves with a zari border and a gold bangle on each bare forearm. Centre-parted hair with a nethi chutti along the parting, jhumkas, a choker, a V haram and an oddiyanam belt. Knees are bent out just past hip width and the shins draw in to small turned-out feet with gold anklets and alta on the toes. A small pleated fan with zari edges hangs between the knees. The braid falls behind the right side with jasmine and a gold kunjalam tassel, and a jasmine strand circles the back of the head.
- **Pose options (`out/dancer_f_pose_options.png`):** A has the left arm out and the right hand raised at the chest (Erika FRLG hand). B has both arms out. **B was picked** because it reads more clearly at 1x. In A the chest hand gets lost against the necklace, and the right arm is cramped.
- **OW:** brown-ramp skin (3/B/C/D), red silk, a gold choker and belt, slightly bent knees with gold cuffs and bells. The pigtails became one braid with jasmine and a tassel, seen on the back and side views.
- **Weak spots:** the thighs plus the fan can still read as a short dress. The OW hair has a maroon tint because npc_2 has no neutral black ramp.
- **Open questions:** keep red and gold, or switch to a contrasting border colour (green or magenta)? Should the alta on the hands be shown?
