LOCATIONS
* Timah City (based on Bukit Timmah) – city in the tropical portion of the region, at the heart of the Tosa Reserve. Gym leader is Hana – she cultivates Orchids in her garden.
* Pokétopia – resort island off the coast accessible via train. Resort is home to the night safari, a marina where players can travel to a number of small islands. Gym leader is Marina – a woman who's mastered the seas.
* Juran City – A popular shopping city where players can travel to Pokétopia via a dedicated train line. The city is home to a number of luxury hotels, and casinos. Here the player uncovers an underground ring of criminals fixing gambling odds and strongarming local small businessses while bigger chains can pay their dues.
* Changu City – The region's international airport is here, bringing in businesspeople from around the world to synergise, innovate and above all make a profit. The gym leader, Larry recently moved here from the Paldea region after being sent over as an expat by Paldea's Champion Geeta to build relationships with Tosa's Pokémon League.
* Faber Town – A town nestled in the cliffs of the Tosa region accessible via cable car or a trecherous climb up the cliff. The leader of the town is Meru, a fierce fighter who trains against Rock Pokémon in the mountains solo.
* Moongrove City – Nestled in the mangroves, gym leader Tork enjoys hunting for catfish.
* Inka City – Gym leader Frea heads up a cold food distribution centre here. Inka City is the domestic logistics hub of the city, and Frea makes sure all the imported food from Hoenn and Johto gets on the plates of the Tosa people. Team Torrent's hidden base is here, with a shipping company as the front (Marlin Co.)
* Bura City – Maria. Cat lady who frequently travels to Juran City for a weekend of gambling. Loves to put her Pokémon in costumes.
* Senoko City – Mo, runs the national power grid. He looks after the wind farms that dot around this town.
* Tosa Port – where our player first arrives. Mainly a domestic port, there is also a cold storage warehouse here.
* Telok Town – where Professor Palm's research laboratory is. A quiet town in the North Tosa Reserve (this is currently called South Tosa Reserve need to change these references in game).
* Tosa Reserve – a sprawling nature reserve at the heart of the Tosa region home to all manner of species. Under the watchful eye of Pokémon Rangers, the reserve has struggled in recent years with ranges of invasive species that have arrived from other regions.
* Kantu Falls – A beautiful series of waterfalls near Faber Town, locals travel here once or twice a year to bathe in the mineral-rich waters.
* Moongrove Marsh – marshlands and mangroves that weave themselves around Moongrove city. Great for fishing up ellusive Pokémon species.
* Corral City – the bustling beach city on Pokétopia where Marina's gym resides. There's also a lovely marina.

To unlock these dungeons players must defeat a Regi Guardian. After the regis disappeared from Team Torrent's HQ they appeared at the entrances of these dungeons, and head inside. Local myths on Tiksi predicted this event, and the guardians were in place to protect the Tiksi natives from these fearsome Pokémon. They will not let the player in, unless the player can defeat them.

Once all of the Regis have been collected, the player must defeat Matt who has captured the Merlion Pokémon at the top of the Tiksi Temple. Matt is defeated, and arrested by the Tosa authorities who arrive with Haru. Haru looks at the Merlion Pokémon, is satisfied with his journey. Having caught so many Pokémon along his journey and battled so many, he now seeks the ultimate trainer and heads off to create the Tosa Battle Park.

The player returns home. A few months pass, and they come back on the boat to Tosa Port. They land, and Professor Palm greets them and hands them the player's Pokémon team who he's been looking after. The player receives a call from Haru who says he heard that he's arrived (the port authority let him know). The player then takes the train to Pokétopia, and arrives at the entrance to the Tosa Battle Park – a collection of five facilties that will push trainers to their limits (this'll just be a reskinned battle frontier for now).

BROAD PLOT
* Team Aqua have returned under the leadership of Matt – the former Aqua admin. Having returned to his homeland of Tosa, Matt and his new Team Torrent have been smuggling Pokémon into the region and selling them to the criminal underworld of Tosa...and some equally shady corporate kingpins. Under the cover of believing that humans are nothing but a plague on the environment, having twisted it for their own benefit, Matt wants to force humans out of the Tosa region selling an environmentally-forward message. In reality, Matt intends to seize control of the region for himself, and to expand his Pokémon smuggling business across the world.

* The player has travelled to the region to work as a post-graduate assistant for Professor Palm. He tasks you with travelling the region to collect data on Pokémon habitats and changes in Pokémon migration patterns as a result of changes in habitats. Over the last five years, the Tosa region has seen a spike in invasive Pokémon species, which has upset the delicate ecosystem of the region. He wants to find out what has caused this (spoilers, it's people).

* As the player travels through the region, they complete the gym challenge as a way of meeting new people and gaining access to broader parts of the Tosa reserve. The region introduced strict restrictions on trainer access to these areas through the gym challenge as they needed to make sure the people entering these sites were knowledgeable and respectful of Pokémon.

* The mythicals of the region are a Merlion fakémon, the guardian of the island. It is said that with it's maw it pulled Tosa away from the neighbouring Hoenn region, and that it slumbers somewhere in the region waiting to protect the region from any threat to its Pokémon.

* On their journey, the player uncovers people looming in the shadows tied to incidents across the Tosa region. These eventually come to a head with a number of encounters with Matt. At the peak – Matt unavails five new Pokémon he's brought over to Tosa Region – the Regi 5. With Regigigas slumbering in Snowpoint Temple, the player has to delve into the ruins deep in the Tosa Reserve to uncover the legendary Merlion Pokémon – the only hope of restoring balance to the region.

* When Matt is defeated, he actually escapes. He is not giving up. Him and his team flee to a region West of Tosa – Tiksi. A rural harsh region with rugged people who look after it. Tosa's authorities do not permit entry for anyone from the Tiksi branch because of historical conflicts between the two regions. You are tasked by the League to travel to the Tiksi branch (you go via a small ship – one of Team Torrent's smuggling ships) and land in a small fishing village on Tiksi. From there, you travel around the island to capture the five regis and take down Matt once and for all on top of Mount Batok.
